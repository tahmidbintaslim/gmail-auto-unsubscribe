# Usability Test Plan: UnsubSweep Website and Extension

## Objectives

1. Can an everyday Gmail user say what UnsubSweep does within 10 seconds of landing on the home page?
2. Do they believe it is safe, and what makes them believe it or doubt it?
3. Can they install it and complete a first sweep without help?
4. Do they understand, before pressing Start, that it leaves every sender and can't be undone?

## Method

Moderated remote usability test, 30 minutes each, screen shared. Think-aloud.

## Participants

6 people who use Gmail on a computer every week, feel they get too many newsletters, and do not work in tech. Mix of ages. Recruit 2 spares. Each needs Chrome and a Gmail account they are willing to use; offer a test account to anyone who isn't.

## Session guide

| Part | Time | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | 3 min | How do you deal with newsletters today? When did you last unsubscribe from something? |
| First impression | 5 min | Show the home page for 10 seconds, then hide it. "What does this do? Who is it for? Would you trust it?" |
| Explore | 7 min | "Find out whether it can read your email." "Find out what it costs." Note where they look and what they skip. |
| Install | 7 min | "Install it." Watch the GitHub install steps; note every stall. |
| First sweep | 5 min | On a test account with 8 subscriptions: "Clear your subscriptions." Before they press Start, ask what they expect to happen. |
| Wrap-up | 3 min | What almost stopped you? What would you tell a friend it does? |

## What we measure

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Says what it does after 10 seconds | 5 of 6 |
| Finds the "never reads your email" answer unaided | 5 of 6 |
| Installs without help | 4 of 6 (GitHub install is the known weak point) |
| Predicts "it leaves all of them, no undo" before pressing Start | 5 of 6 |
| Would recommend (yes / maybe / no) | Record, no target |

## Known risks to probe

- Installing from GitHub with "Load unpacked" is hard for non-technical people. Expect this to fail until the Chrome Web Store listing is live.
- "No undo" may not be noticed. Watch whether anyone reads the line under the first panel.
- "Pro: In development" may read as a paywall on the free version.

## Synthesis

After the sessions: group observations by theme, rank by how many people hit each problem and how badly, and turn the top five into changes. Keep one quote per theme.
