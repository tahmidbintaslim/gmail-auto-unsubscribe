Gmail Auto Unsubscribe: demo

The extension's own popup.html, popup.js, unsubscriber.js and content.js, run against the test fixture in test/fixtures/, a hand-written stand-in for Gmail. demo.js plays the parts of the chrome.* APIs and of Gmail's dialogs. Nothing on this page talks to Google or to any sender, and a "Go to website" click is counted but opens nothing.
