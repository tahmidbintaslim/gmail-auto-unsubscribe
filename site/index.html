Bulk unsubscribe from Gmail. Without giving anyone your inbox.

UnsubSweep works through Gmail's own Manage subscriptions page for you, sender by sender, right in your browser.

Add to Chrome, it's freeTry the demo

Open source. Two permissions. Nothing to sign in to.

Zero access

No Google sign-in, no OAuth, no API. It clicks in the Gmail tab you already have open.

Zero servers

There is no backend. The extension makes no network requests of its own.

Zero data

It never reads your email and stores nothing. Read every line on GitHub.
